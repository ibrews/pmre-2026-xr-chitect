ARCHITECT TO XR-CHITECT — FREE SPATIAL MEDIA STARTER GUIDE
Alex Coulombe Presents · PMRE 2026 · November 19, Las Vegas

1. Choose one room you can legally capture and share. Start with a space you
   know well so you can spot missing details, scale errors and misleading views.
2. Capture overlapping views of the room, moving slowly with steady light.
   Try Scaniverse for capture; Polycam is another option (plan limits apply).
   Read the vendor's current capture instructions and privacy settings first.
3. Review the result from several viewpoints. Check thin objects, mirrors,
   windows, doorways, missing surfaces and anything that could mislead a buyer.
4. Use SuperSplat to review, clean up and frame a small Gaussian-splat scene.
   Keep your original capture. Export a browser-deliverable version, then test
   it on the client's actual phone or computer before promising compatibility.
5. Build one useful deliverable: a browser tour, a short orbit film or a set of
   additional views. Never use generated content to invent a property's facts.
6. For VR or mixed reality, check viewer/file support, scale, performance,
   comfort, permissions and device controls. A browser tour is not automatically
   a polished multi-user headset app. Test the exact target viewer and headset.
7. Price the complete service: capture + processing + review + hosting + delivery
   + revisions. Package it with the photography or film you already sell.
8. For unbuilt luxury projects, hosted VR, photoreal Unreal, physical stagecraft
   or custom interaction: talk to Agile Lens about collaborating.
