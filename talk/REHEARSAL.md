### 28. Who painted this?

⏱ ~75s.

Three deliberate reveals: (0) left painting only, ask “Anyone know who painted this?” (1) reveal right painting, ask “And this one?” (2) reveal dates and ages: 1896 / age 14–15, 1972 / age 90. Both are Picasso. Let the room answer before revealing the next image or the dates.

After the hiring/curiosity story: learn the disciplines before changing their rules. Passion benefits from deliberate practice. Psychologist K. Anders Ericsson, with Ralf Krampe and Clemens Tesch-Römer, developed the deliberate-practice framework in 1993: focused, effortful work on specific weaknesses, tasks just beyond current ability, feedback and correction, often guided by a qualified teacher. Repetition alone is not the definition. Ericsson and Robert Pool explain the framework in Peak (2016); it is not a term originating in Atomic Habits.
