After the hiring/curiosity story: learn the disciplines before changing their rules. Passion benefits from deliberate practice. Psychologist K. Anders Ericsson, with Ralf Krampe and Clemens Tesch-Römer, developed the deliberate-practice framework in 1993: focused, effortful work on specific weaknesses, tasks just beyond current ability, feedback and correction, often guided by a qualified teacher.
