### 28. Who painted this?

⏱ ~75s.

Three deliberate reveals: (0) left painting only, ask “Anyone know who painted this?” (1) reveal right painting, ask “And this one?” (2) reveal dates 1896 / 1972. Both are Picasso. Let the room answer before revealing the next image or the dates.

After the hiring/curiosity story: learn the disciplines before changing their rules. Passion benefits from deliberate practice. Psychologist K. Anders Ericsson, with Ralf Krampe and Clemens Tesch-Römer, developed the deliberate-practice framework in 1993: focused, effortful work on specific weaknesses, tasks just beyond current ability, feedback and correction, often guided by a qualified teacher. Repetition alone is not the definition. Ericsson and Robert Pool explain the framework in Peak (2016); it is not a term originating in Atomic Habits.

Sources: Ericsson, Krampe & Tesch-Römer (1993), The Role of Deliberate Practice in the Acquisition of Expert Performance, Psychological Review100(3),363–406, https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-295X.100.3.363 and original paper https://www.moodle.is.ed.ac.uk/pluginfile.php/29313/mod_resource/content/2/ericsson%201993.pdf; Peak (Ericsson & Pool,2016).
