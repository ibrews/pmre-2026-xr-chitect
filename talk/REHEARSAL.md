### Thesis alternates

- **a** — The Advice Wasn’t Wrong. / It Just Assumed One Lane.: Master your craft — absolutely. I did specialize: in a niche nobody had named yet.  The lane worth staying in is the one you build where your crafts overlap.
- **b** — Specialize in / the Overlap.: Master a craft — then find where it touches another one. That overlap is a lane nobody else is in.  Mine was architecture, theater, and a headset from Kickstarter.
- **d** — Master the Craft. / Then Trust Your Taste.: A year with no computers taught me what a line is worth. Every tool since — Revit, VR, AI — has been faster. None of them decide what’s worth doing.  That part is still yours.
- **c** — Pick a Lane. / Then Build a Bridge.: The advice was half right. Depth matters.  But the bridges between crafts are where the new work lives — and where nobody is competing with you yet.
